# Test steps: market events lesson and rival name

1. Sign in as the instructor and open a class. Click **View as student** for any team, then open **Learn**.
   - You should see **Market events: responding to shocks** (companion lesson for Market news) above the Creative lesson, with an instructor note at the bottom.
2. Sign in as a student in a class that is in the Practice round, Round 1 or Round 2 (default schedule).
   - The Learn tab should NOT show the market events lesson yet.
3. In a class where Round 3 or later is open (the showcase class works after "Rebuild on current rules"), sign in as a student or guest.
   - Home shows **Market news**, and its last line names the lesson on the Learn tab.
   - Learn shows the market events lesson, open, with no instructor note and no event sizes.
4. Round 5 or later: the Market news item and the segment panels name the new rival **Orvane** (it used to say Zenra).
5. Optional: on the class page, under Market rules, turn off all three events. The lesson should no longer appear for students.
