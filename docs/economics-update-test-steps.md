# Economics update: test steps

Use a fresh demo class so old rounds don't mix with the new rules. Old classes still work, but rounds already played keep their old numbers.

**Setup**
1. Wait for the Vercel deploy after merging (a minute or two).
2. Open Your classes and click **Create demo class**. It comes with 3 rounds already played under the new rules, so Round 3 is open.
3. To change decisions, sign out and sign in as one of the fake students in another browser window (the shared password is on the class screen). **View as student** is read-only. To score a round, use **Close and score ... now** on the class screen.
4. Step 2 (spending limit) checks Round 1 numbers. In the demo class you'll see the Round 3 limit instead: the greater of $500K or 20% of the team's Round 2 revenue.

**1. How costs work**
1. Go to **Start here**, then step 1 (scenario briefing). Scroll to **How costs work**, just after "Where the money comes from".
2. Check that it says parts get 2% cheaper every round, and lists the milestones of 50,000, 150,000 and 300,000 units (95%, 90%, 85%).
3. Check that the money section says "$2,000,000 to start ... then you earn more by selling", and mentions the spending limit and the HQ loan.
4. Go to **Decisions**, then Products. Under the quote, look for the line "Costs fall over time. See How costs work ...".

**2. Spending limit**
1. In Decisions, look at the money rail on the right. Under the marketing budget it should say **This round's spending limit** with an amount (in Round 1 that is $500K).
2. Go to Price & promotion and add promotion until promotion plus research is over $500K. The limit line turns red. On Review, a red notice says promotion will be scaled down.
3. After the round runs, Results shows a note that promotion was cut to fit the spending limit.

**3. Goals**
1. On Strategy & goals, find the line "A goal left blank scores 0 in Goal accuracy."
2. Leave one goal blank for Team 1 and fill in all goals for Team 2. After the round, Team 1's Goals points should be lower.
3. In Start here, step 4 (scoring), the Goal accuracy row says a blank goal scores 0.

**4. Region exit (Round 2 or later)**
1. On Markets & channels, an entered region now shows **Exit this region this round** with its fee (half the entry fee).
2. Tick it. A warning explains what happens. On Review, the capital check shows "Region exit fees".
3. After the round: Results shows the exit fee on the profit and loss, plus a note that you exited and that leftover stock was cleared. Next round, the region shows "Enter this round" again. Entering costs the full fee.

**5. Channel volume discounts**
1. On Markets & channels, the note under the channel table says reopening a channel starts over on volume discounts.

**6. HQ emergency loan**
1. For one team, set production very high (for example 3 times the forecast in every region) and add a large capacity expansion. Run the round, then repeat for another round.
2. When capital runs out, Results shows a note that HQ lent money, a "You owe HQ an emergency loan" concern and "HQ loan owed". The money rail shows the loan in red. Capital never shows below $0.
3. In the next round, the profit and loss shows **Interest on HQ loan**.

**7. Financial score**
1. On Results, under Operating profit, look for **Profit for your Financial score** and the line explaining that one-time costs are left out and expansion is spread over 4 rounds.
2. In a round where a team enters a region or launches a product, the Financial score profit should be higher than Operating profit by about those fees.
3. In Start here, step 4, the Financial row describes the new rule.

**8. Trade support**
1. In a round's Promotion results (Results page), the stocking column for partner channels should show a lower "before" number than in older rounds. With $0 of trade promotion, mass retail and pharmacies reach about half as many buyers.

If anything looks off, tell me which step and what you saw.
