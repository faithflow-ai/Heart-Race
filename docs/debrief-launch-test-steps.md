# Test steps: Round 1 debrief slide "How a rival launched"

Use the Vercel preview link on the PR, or the live site after merging.

1. Sign in as the instructor and open a class that has Round 1 scored. A fresh **demo class** works (Your classes → Create demo class).
2. On the class screen, open **Debrief** and pick **Round 1** in the Round menu.
3. A slide called **How a rival launched** appears after "Who won each segment".
4. The first row is the AI rival with the highest Round 1 score (shaded). Below it is every student team.
5. Each row shows the regions entered, the channels used, the average fit (0 to 100) of each product with its own target segment, whether prices differ by region (with the price range), and the Round 1 score.
6. The discussion questions under the slide are questions only, with no answers.
7. Pick **Round 2** or later in the Round menu: the slide is not there (it's a Round 1 slide only).
